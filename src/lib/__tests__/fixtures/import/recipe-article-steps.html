These bowls take about thirty minutes and make good lunches all week long.

You will need 300 g chicken thighs, 1 cup jasmine rice, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 lemon, and 2 cloves garlic.

1. Rinse the rice and simmer it in 1.5 cups water for 15 minutes with the lid on.

2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and the zest of half the lemon, then sear it 5 minutes a side.

3. Slice the chicken, squeeze the lemon over the bowls and serve with the rice.
